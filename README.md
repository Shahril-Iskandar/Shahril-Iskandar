# Hi there, I'm Shahril 👋

I am a PhD candidate at Griffith University working in biomechanics and musculoskeletal modelling. My research focuses on personalised walking strategies for people with hip osteoarthritis using neuromusculoskeletal modelling.

Previously, I worked on research involving running biomechanics, wearable sensors, human pose estimation, and exoskeletons.

## Research interests

- Biomechanics and gait analysis
- Musculoskeletal and neuromusculoskeletal modelling
- Wearable sensors and motion capture
- Personalised rehabilitation and biofeedback
- Data analysis and research software

## Tools

`MATLAB` · `Python` · `R` · `OpenSim`
